Orbit Lab

Fling planets around a star and watch Newton's law of gravitation and Kepler's three laws show up live, with real numbers.

  • Slingshot a planet: drag on empty space, pull back, let go. Green path = bound orbit, orange = escape.
  • Press 3 for four planets, then watch the T² ÷ a³ column settle near 1.00.
  • Press K on an ellipse to see equal areas swept in equal times.
