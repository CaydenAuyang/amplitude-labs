Amplitude Labs

Four interactive physics-engine labs for four departments. Each one turns a real classroom idea into something you can grab, fling and watch unfold. Built with Matter.js.

01 Physics

Orbit Lab

Fling planets around a star and watch Newton's gravity and Kepler's three laws play out live.

Open lab → 02 Music

Gravity Piano

A piano played by falling notes. Intervals, chords, scales and frequencies light up as it plays.

Open lab → 03 World Languages

Lingo Drop

Spanish and French word cards rain down. Sort them by meaning, gender and conjugation, and hear every word aloud.

Open lab → 04 Statistics

Galton Lab

Every ball is a coin-flip experiment. Watch the binomial become a bell curve, with the stats drawn on top.
