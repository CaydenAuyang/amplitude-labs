Amplitude (n.): how far a wave swings. Move across the line. A = 0.0 px

Four labs for four departments, built in one class period.

Team Amplitude: Cayden, Christoph, Ronan and James H.

↓
Labs(04)
01 Orbit Lab Physics

Fling planets around a star and watch Newton's gravity and Kepler's three laws show up in the numbers.

 ↗ 02 Gravity Piano Music

A piano played by falling notes. Intervals, chords, scales and frequencies appear as it plays.

 ↗ 03 Lingo Drop World Languages

Spanish and French word cards fall into a pile. Sort them by theme, gender and conjugation, and hear each one aloud.

 ↗ 04 Galton Lab Statistics

Every ball is a coin-flip experiment. The bins fill into a bell curve, with the statistics drawn on top.

 ↗

Every lab runs on Matter.js, a 2D physics engine. Press X in any lab to see the real bodies, springs and contacts under the drawing.
